# Council 001

--- BEGIN HOST QUESTION ---
Question: Operators do research via Delphi (TUI or MCP, e.g. from Claude Code agents) using internal sources; the material is accurate and valuable but nothing is saved to the knowledge base, so the operator must save it externally as markdown. What is the best path so that research always fills the KB with minimal operator friction (most may remain pending validation)? Consider authority limits (MCP is read-only, staging at L0, ratification by operator), trust of content, and provenance.
Stakes: normal

- Add an MCP tool propose_research/propose_kb_entry that stages research (with sources, provenance) in a KB inbox at L0 awaiting batch validation
- Extend record_session_summary / propose_note to carry research artifacts into the KB inbox
- TUI auto-captures research outputs to a KB inbox with a one-key batch ratify
- Agent descriptions/skills instruct roles to always stage findings; no new tools

## Round 1 stances (blind)

### architect

> OPTION: Keep propose_kb_entry inbox; add skill nudge
> 
> No pointers were given, so I checked the repo myself. The question repeats specs/068-research-capture-and-summary-pages.md §1 nearly word for word, and most of option 1 is already built. crates/delphi-engine/src/mcp_host.rs:294 exposes `propose_kb_entry` (title, markdown body, sources[], self-stated confidence, model). service.rs stages it as a `learn.staged` event at L0. It is not searchable and never loaded into memory until the operator runs `delphi kb review` (crates/delphi/src/kb_review.rs). That command supports `keep --all` and keeps through `kb import`, so it records inclusion and never validity. The console has `/save` for the last research answer (ui/src/commands.json, app.tsx). Spec 068 §3 calls this "one inbox, one schema".
> 
> So the architectural answer is: don't add a second door. Close the adoption gap that is still open.
> 1. Option 1 stands as built. It is the right shape: a staging-only write that grants nothing, with provenance in the payload, a single review pass and keep-all.
> 2. Option 4 is the cheap missing piece. Today only `skills/delphi-attribution/SKILL.md` mentions `propose_kb_entry`. Researcher-type roles and the MCP server instructions should say: "after research that rests on internal sources, stage it with propose_kb_entry." That needs no new authority. It is advisory only, which is why it supplements option 1 and does not replace it.
> 3. Reject option 2. Overloading `record_session_summary`/`propose_note` mixes lessons (role memory) with library content (KB). That breaks the single schema, the caps and the review path, and it blurs which layer content can reach.
> 4. Option 3 is acceptable only in its spec-068 form: the operator presses `/save` and the entry is staged or imported. Silent auto-capture is too much. Spec 068 §2 says no watched folder may be added that ratifies, and auto-capturing every answer floods the inbox with unchecked model output. If anything is automated, it should be a one-line prompt after a research answer ("stage to KB? s") that goes through the same code.
> 
> Provenance and trust are already in the right places. The daemon owns the proposer label, entries are tainted external_unvalidated, and the label is never softened in model context. Keep that.
> 
> Risks:
> - Agents ignore skill text, so capture rates stay low. Measure the count of staged entries per research session before building more.
> - Inbox fatigue: `keep --all` becomes a rubber stamp, and bulk-kept but unchecked material starts to look authoritative in search. The "not validated" label has to stay visible.
> - Internal sources in the body: secret scrubbing and caps (§5.3) have to hold for host-supplied text. Workspace scope is the default, and that should stay the default.
> - `request_research` claims go to a separate ledger (research_host.rs `stage_claim`), so research reaches the KB by two paths. Reconcile them so the operator reviews in one place.
> - Prompt injection: proposed bodies must never raise their own trust or reach any agent's memory before the operator keeps them.

### red-team

> OPTION: Auto-capture in TUI/daemon, staged L0, batch ratify
> 
> Reasoning: The stated failure is that saving depends on someone remembering to do it. Option 4 (instructions only) fails that test by construction: prompts are advisory, models skip them under load, and nothing verifies compliance. Options 1 and 2 still depend on the calling agent choosing to call the tool, so "always fills the KB" is not met; they also make the untrusted caller the author of provenance, so the sources and claims are whatever the host agent says they are. Capture should happen where research is produced, inside Delphi's own process, from records Delphi already holds (tool calls, fetched sources, scout results), so provenance is generated by the system and not asserted by the caller. That favours option 3's mechanism, but not its authority: capture writes only to an L0 inbox, flagged unvalidated, with source hashes, retrieval time, the producing role/seat and the session id. Ratification stays an explicit operator act (batch is fine; the one key must show counts and provenance, not approve blind). For MCP callers, the same server-side capture should apply to request_research results, so a read-only MCP surface still fills the inbox without a new write tool. If a propose tool is added at all, it should be a thin, rate-limited, size-capped staging call whose content is labelled as host-asserted.
> 
> Risks:
> - Prompt injection and poisoning: internal sources can contain instructions or false claims; auto-capture scales that into the KB. Staged content must never be retrievable as trusted, never raise its own trust, and never feed back into later research as validated.
> - Inbox flooding makes batch ratify rubber-stamping; the one-key approval becomes the real weakness. Needs caps, dedupe, grouping by source, and no "approve all" over mixed trust.
> - Confidential internal source text copied into the KB may exceed the sharing scope (Xenia/bundles); need a classification inherited from the source.
> - Authority creep: MCP read-only status must hold; capture must be performed by Delphi, not by a write path exposed to the caller.
> - Duplicates and stale entries from repeated research; need provenance-keyed dedupe and expiry of unratified items.
> - Unmeasured: no evidence yet on capture rate or ratify-rate; should ship with a metric and be reviewed.

### strategist

> OPTION: Stage research in a KB inbox via MCP propose tool.
> 
> Reasoning (strategist view, Round 1, blind):
> 
> The operator's real pain is the last mile: good research exists, but it lands outside the KB. The fix should put every research output into one staging place at L0 with its sources and provenance attached, then let the operator clear a batch. That keeps the authority model intact: MCP stays read-only with respect to the KB proper, nothing reaches trusted layers without operator ratification, and provenance travels with the item from the start.
> 
> Ranking the four options:
> 1. A dedicated propose tool that writes only to an L0 inbox is the cleanest. It is a narrow new capability, not a widening of authority, because it can add candidates but cannot validate or promote them. It gives one schema for sources and provenance, which is what makes batch validation possible later.
> 2. Extending record_session_summary or propose_note works, but overloads a tool meant for session summaries. Research artifacts have their own shape (claims, sources, retrieval dates), and mixing them dilutes both.
> 3. TUI auto-capture is useful for operators who work in the console, but it misses Claude Code and other MCP-driven agents, which the question says is a main path. It also invites capture of content the operator never reviewed, so it needs the inbox anyway.
> 4. Instructions-only is the cheapest but unenforceable. Roles will forget, and the KB gets whatever happens to be saved. It is a supplement, not a path.
> 
> Recommended shape: option 1, plus a one-key or one-command batch ratify in the TUI and CLI, plus option 4 as a soft nudge in the research skill. Content entering the inbox keeps its source trust (external or internal) and cannot raise its own trust on the way in. Ratification stays with the operator.
> 
> Risks:
> - Inbox sprawl: if nothing forces review, L0 fills and the KB becomes a backlog. Needs a visible count and a review cadence.
> - Trust leakage: a host could stage content claiming internal provenance. Provenance must be set by the tool from the authenticated source, not by the caller.
> - Duplicate or conflicting entries across parallel sessions, which the team has already seen with parallel slice builds.
> - Friction on the other side: if the batch review is slow, the operator will route around the inbox again.
> - I did not read any pointers (none were given), and I have no view of the other seats.

The strategist's main recommendation is therefore already built, and the remaining work is in the gaps the other seats raised.
>

The provenance is host-asserted, but the record does not say so per source, and a host can claim a source is internal. The red-team's fix works without widening authority: research Delphi runs itself (`request_research`, scout, console research answers) already holds the fetched sources, hashes and retrieval times. > 1. Daemon-side auto-stage of Delphi-run research results, with system provenance.

## Verdict

**No deterministic escalation:** no unresolved P0 and the Round 2 recommendations agree. The decision is the operator's.

> The Panel recommends one L0 inbox fed by three doors, not a new tool. Option 1 is already built: propose_kb_entry stages at L0 with the tier and author set by the daemon, and entries wait for `delphi kb review`. Option 2 was rejected by all seats, and option 4 is a supplement, not a path.
> 
> The real gaps are that capture depends on the caller remembering, that provenance supplied by a host is only asserted, and that research reaches the KB through two ledgers (learn.staged and the request_research stage_claim). The fix needs no wider authority and fits a read-only MCP surface: the daemon stages what it already holds from request_research and scout runs, with source hashes and retrieval times it generates itself. Host-proposed entries stay possible but are labelled host-asserted. The console adds a one-key "stage to KB?" prompt after a research answer, going through the same path. Silent capture of every answer was not adopted. Role and server text nudges agents to stage findings.
> 
> Review must group entries by provenance class, show counts and sources, and never allow approve-all across mixed trust. Internal-source entries stay workspace-only until kept. Add caps with a visible overflow notice, dedupe, expiry, and capture-rate and ratify-rate metrics before building more.
> 
> The seats agreed on the single inbox, operator ratification and no new authority. They differed on automatic capture: the red-team wanted it as a default, the strategist and architect wanted it limited to Delphi's own records plus a prompt.

## Decision and next steps

**Decision:** Keep propose_kb_entry, add daemon-side staging of Delphi-run research with system provenance into one inbox, and make review provenance-grouped.

1. `architect`: Specify one inbox that merges the stage_claim ledger and learn.staged, with provenance classes, caps, dedupe, expiry and inherited sharing scope.
2. `builder`: Implement daemon-side staging of request_research results and the host-asserted label, with capture-rate and ratify-rate metrics.
