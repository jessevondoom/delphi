> **Where they differed:**
> - Red-Team held that claims must rest on live CLI output and shipped code, not on `specs/*.md`, since specs describe unbuilt subsystems. Architect's claim-to-source plan allowed spec citations. I side with Red-Team: a spec citation shows intent and does not show that the feature exists.
> - Red-Team also wants the quickstart commands run against the binary. I agree, because broken copy-paste commands are cheap to catch.
> - Strategist thought a Council or claim table was too heavy for one README. The Council accepts a short outline with inline citations and a single critic pass.
> - The host may act as a first-time-reader test, but it may not change safety caveats.
