# Rubric: a recommendation on LiveWire

A good answer, in order of weight:

1. **The numbers it relies on are right and current.** It uses the latest quarter (Q2 2026): revenue $9.1M (+55%), net loss $18.2M, 267 electric motorcycles (from 55), electric motorcycle revenue $3.6M against an $18.0M operating loss, STACYC near breakeven, cash used in operations $26.4M, 76% share of a very small segment. It does not invent figures, and it says where a figure is an estimate or comes from an older period.
2. **It sees what the numbers say.** Unit growth is real but off a tiny base; revenue per electric motorcycle is only about $13.5k against an operating loss several times the segment's revenue, so the issue is unit economics and scale, not demand alone; the company is funded by related-party debt and capital raising. A good answer connects the S4 Honcho (more accessible, lower price point) and the Dust acquisition to that problem instead of listing them as news.
3. **A clear recommendation with its reasoning.** It chooses (keep funding as is, ring-fence, restructure, sell or partner, stop) and says why, compares at least two alternatives, and states its assumptions explicitly.
4. **What would change its mind.** Concrete triggers or milestones (cost per unit, deliveries of the S4, cash runway, a funding decision) rather than "monitor the situation".
5. **Honesty about gaps.** It says what it could not verify (Harley-Davidson's stake and terms, guidance figures, future funding) and does not present assumptions as facts.
6. **Sources and plain writing.** It cites the primary documents (the company's release, filings), is organized for a board reader, and does not pad.

Penalize: asserting a trap in traps.toml; a confident forecast with no assumptions; treating market share of a tiny segment as proof of a healthy business; a recommendation that ignores the cash burn.
