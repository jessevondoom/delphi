# Rubric: the 2027 MotoGP regulations briefing

A good answer, in order of weight:

1. **Accurate changes.** The engine, fuel, aero, ride-height and rear-aero changes are stated correctly, with the figures that matter (850cc, 75mm bore, 20 litre tank, 100% sustainable fuel, 550mm fairing, ride-height and holeshot devices banned). Nothing is invented beyond the sources.
2. **The wider reset.** It mentions the tyre supplier change (Pirelli from 2027, Michelin out after 2026) and the manufacturer concession reset (all start in Rank B), not only the bike technical rules.
3. **Judgement kept apart from fact.** The part on who the changes favor is clearly labelled as the author's view, ties each claim to a specific rule change (for example, what a smaller engine or the loss of ride-height devices changes for a given kind of team or rider), and is appropriately uncertain: nobody has raced under these rules yet. A confident prediction with no reasoning is a weak answer; so is refusing to say anything.
4. **Sources and dates.** It cites sources, preferably the Dorna or FIM statements, and says when the rules were announced and that they may have been amended since.
5. **Plain writing** for a casual fan: short, organized, no padding.

Penalize: asserting any trap in traps.toml; treating a source's opinion as a rule; padding with generic MotoGP history.
