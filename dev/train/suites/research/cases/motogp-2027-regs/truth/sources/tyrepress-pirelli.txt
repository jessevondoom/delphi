Date: 6th March 2025
Pirelli will be the exclusive official tyre supplier to MotoGP from 2027. A new five-year contract until 2031 inclusive, is currently being finalised. According to an official statement on the matter published by Dorna, which runs MotoGP, the new five-year deal will “coincide with a new era for the sport as new MotoGP bikes and technical regulations debut in 2027”. Michelin will remain the exclusive supplier for MotoGP and MotoE until the end of the current technical regulations, at the close of the 2026 season.
Over the next two seasons Michelin will continue to provide its technical support as well as “its products and world class technology, assuring the safety, performance and racing…”. Michelin will remain committed to MotoE throughout 2025 and 2026.
Dorna described the strategy of having a single supplier in the paddock as “the perfect development ladder for the champions of tomorrow to hone their craft on their way to the top”. Further details on the tyre allocations and specifications to be supplied by Pirelli are expected to follow.
