Built 2026-11-21 (pull request #854, not merged): `forge_tools.rs` (read lists as data, `[forge.tools.<harness>] read`, which can only pick from the CLI's known read tools; the gate: on the list, every path absolute and inside the copy through links and never under `.git`, no path-less call; the version store `local/forge-tools.json` with probed-pass, probed-fail, grace and unprobed; a 5-day grace only after an update from a passed version, a never-probed version tool-less until its probe passes; the watchdog; the probe behind the `CarrierProbe` trait and a live implementation, one small call per version: one allowed read, one refused write). Review seats (`host.review`) are forge readers of the review copy: Claude with `--tools` set to the list and an empty working directory, so every read comes to `harness_decide` first; agy through its `PreToolUse` hook (`HookPolicy::with_forge`). Edit and shell calls are refused at the gate (a reader has no approval bridge); nothing is put to the operator. A copy with a link out of it keeps the seat tool-less. The daemon's timer probes one due version when idle (operator quiet 5 minutes, no task), logs the cost (`forge.tools_probe` and the record), and turns a newly bridge-only version into a console notice; `delphi doctor` check `11 forge tools`. The watchdog stops a seat whose own call ran without passing the gate, drops the version, logs `forge_tool_ungated` and `forge.tools_dropped`. Bridge-only by design: **codex** (no per-tool allow or deny flag), **copilot** (whether ACP asks before a read outside its working directory is unprobed, spec 132 C8). Verified: AC-17 (scripted Claude seat and agy hook client), AC-18 (store and background probe with a fake clock and fake carrier; an unprobed version stays tool-less and is left due), AC-19 (scripted stream), AC-20 (bridge with no answer; reader policy). Hardened after an independent security review of the pull request (same day, second commit): the gate refuses a call with any argument it cannot judge (an argument key not on a per-tool list, a nested value, more than 16 patterns; nothing past a cap goes unchecked, the review's HIGH finding), a glob with `..` anywhere (brace expansion too), and a `file:` URI in any argument; the Claude watchdog ignores calls whose paths all lie in the seat's own empty working directory; a copy with a hard-linked file keeps the seat tool-less; a probe passes only when the refused write was seen refused (else undecided; the Claude probe is offered `Write` for that); a first probe fail is retried once after 24 hours, a second is final; a watchdog drop stays with its version while a new version starts fresh. Re-check follow-ups (third commit): tests that the working-directory exemption does not reach past it (`..`, a symlink out, a `-evil` sibling); an undecided probe is retried at most once a day and the third undecided result gives the version up (bridge-only, no grace).
