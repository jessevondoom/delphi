| 116 | G6 | consolidation: the mechanical pass, then the model pass as proposals; episode expiry | built and merged (#736): `[memory] consolidate` (default false; "mechanical"; true or "model") and `consolidate_tokens` (30,000); once a day after five idle minutes with no task running. Episodes get `valid_until` 180 days and a resolution supersedes earlier unresolved episodes of the same first error fingerprint in sibling sessions.
