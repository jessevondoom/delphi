| 118 | P5 | figures in pages: the figure spec, rendering, the label, the number and label check | built and merged (#748): the writer asks for a figure with a fenced `figure` block holding one JSON object in the 095 chart or diagram form (`type`, `title`, inline `data` rows or `nodes`/`edges`) plus `from`, the claim or source markers it rests on (at most 20, at most 6 figures a page; `data.from` files and a `source` line are refused). The daemon resolves the markers at the page's scope (a private one fails an external page), renders with the existing `delphi_docs` chart and diagram code to PNG, stores it in `library/figures/` with a record (`licence: "generated"`, `made_by` the writer's role ID, `markers`, kind, title, blake3, width, height) and puts it in the page as a picture at `lib:_figures/` plus the hash; `finish_page` writes `Figure made by <role> from [markers]. Not from a source.` under it, a writer's own such line is refused and `pages check` fails a figure without the exact line. The check: every number in a chart's data and in the text it draws (title, subtitle, column names, annotations) must equal a whole number token of a cited claim's text or a cited source's stored text (so `1` is never found inside `2019`); every diagram node label must appear as whole words in the page text outside figure blocks. A failing figure is dropped, the failure (kind, title, reason, one plain line) goes to the page sidecar, the validator verdict and the `figure_dropped` log entry, and `pages show` prints it. A `lib:_figures/` address the writer typed is dropped, so a figure is never reused unchecked on another page. The image method serves a figure with its record and label instead of a picture credit; `assets check` covers `figures/`. First draft by Gemini via agy, reviewed and revised. Left: figures are not carried by `library export` (per-source); figures of a draft that later failed stay on disk with their records; a diagram's edge labels and sequence messages are not checked (§5 item 3 names node labels); "add to a topic" for mission artifacts (§5 item 5) |
