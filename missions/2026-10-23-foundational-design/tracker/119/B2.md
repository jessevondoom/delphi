| 119 | B2 | `[library] unknown_licence` (default `excerpts`), private text, the 300-character rule and its scan test, purge, list, the cap | built and merged (#734): a research run keeps each passage it quoted from a page it does not hold whole (at most 300 characters, up to 200 either side) as an excerpt, and the claim's anchor is an ordinary one into it; the passages (never their context) are indexed as `library/sources/<id>/excerpts.md`. **Anchors already written** (112 R2, quote alone, `via: research`) stay as they are and readable; the next time a run reads that page under `excerpts`, every claim citing it whose quote is on the page gains the passage and the anchor is replaced in place (nothing is fetched for this alone; a recheck does not do it). The library's 10% bound is not applied to these passages (10% of a short page is shorter than one quote); 16 KiB a source is, counted with the context. `private_text` keeps such a page whole as a private source marked `kept_privately`, unless it shows a rights notice the code detects or its trusted-list entry allows a summary only (§3 item 3 says `reuse = none`; the list's word is `summary-only`); a page may carry at most 300 characters of it in one run (a page-check finding over the whole body, for cited `kept_privately` sources); its bibliography says `kept privately`; the cap removes the text last read or checked longest ago (no read is recorded, so `last_checked`).
