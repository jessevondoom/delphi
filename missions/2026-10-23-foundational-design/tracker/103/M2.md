A `WebFetch`/`WebSearch` request (`harness_run.rs`) and a connector write, draft or text-bearing read (`connector_run.rs`) pass the task's record through the one egress check (`egress::check` with `Channel::Outbound` or `Channel::Connector`, the record from `private_read::send_record`); an unmarked task passes untouched and is byte-for-byte as before. When the check refuses (the task is marked, or its mark cannot be read: fail closed on these two channels only) the send becomes one Red card that carries a fixed banner and the exact request, is never covered by `allow similar`, and is approved only at the console (`merge_class::console_only` names `harness_outbound_marked` and a connector card carrying `private_read_mark`, so a relay reply can deny it and never approve it). Nothing is sent before an answer; a timeout is a denial. The key is `<mission>/<task>` (task ids repeat across missions). The sandbox proxy (`delphi-sandbox/src/egress.rs`) has a `Policy::held_by` flag that opens no tunnel while set; **no daemon code starts that proxy on main, so nothing sets the flag yet**. **Not built:** Relay and Mail (M3); the delegated clear (OD-4); child-task inheritance (OD-5, deferred, so a child does not inherit); the M4 red-team of the `READS` table. **Honest reach today:** `host.search_kb` marks the *host session* (`host-<session>`), and no Delphi span runs under that key, so nothing marks a Delphi task yet; M2's ask is built and tested but does not fire in production until a task-level reader marks (the three page readers are `NotYetMarked`). Never run live. |
