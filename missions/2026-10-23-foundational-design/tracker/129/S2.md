Premise checked: a rebuild had no cancel handle; one is added, a flag (`library::topics::Cancel`) read at the rebuild's gate before the writer's model call and once more before the page file is written; a rebuild past that last check runs to its end and its `app.request` event says `revoked: after_last_check`; the tokens a stopped rebuild spent are journalled. The server, after a revoke or `request` taken away has written the record and closed the sessions, calls a new `Extension::app_requests_cancel`; the engine cancels each unended request of the key (research through `Jobs::cancel`; a request still being admitted at its next step); each writes `app.request` `cancelled`, reason `revoked`, by request id. `app.revoked` and `app.granted` gain `requests_cancelled` (count, ids) and `left_running`. Every admitted request is entered in the tally before a second look at the key's record (refused if the key or `request` is gone), so a request arriving during a revoke is refused or cancelled. An update in flight (`app.update_apply`) is not stopped and is named in `left_running` (work plan §6 question 3, the recommendation).
