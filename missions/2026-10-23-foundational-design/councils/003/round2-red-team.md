### 2. Conflict Resolution

- **(a) Operator Permission-Widening:** **Keep `default_access_scope` (`read`, `write`, `execute`) strictly narrow-only (reject `delphi customize grant`); allow `authorized_skills` in overrides to add ratified custom/operator skills whose `required_permissions` are a subset of the built-in role's `default_access_scope` ([001-governance-and-manifests.md](../../../../specs/001-governance-and-manifests.md#L87)).**
  *Justification:* Widening `default_access_scope` lets non-builder roles (`orchestrator`, `architect`, `red-team`) mutate code or run shells outside `IsolatedBuild`, bypassing `No Spec, No Build`, the 5 Merge Gates ([003-collaboration-and-council.md](../../../../specs/003-collaboration-and-council.md#L54-L69)), and critic independence ([GOVERNANCE.md](../../../../GOVERNANCE.md#L29-L44), `[REV-SEC-02]`), while an `operator/ratified.lock` waiver silently escalates privileges across every workspace on the machine. Allowing scope-subset custom/operator skills in `authorized_skills` (and routing code-mutating workflows through `@builder`) preserves operator extensibility without ever raising a role's privilege ceiling.
- **(b) D-19 Sequencing:** **Agree with Strategist's Step 2 / Step 3 / Step 3B split.**
  *Justification:* Splitting [009-model-access-and-auth.md](../../../../specs/009-model-access-and-auth.md#L175-L181) across **Step 2** (endpoint selection/pinning, `keychain`/`env`/`command`, Claude Direct + `gateway-native`, ledger columns), **Step 3** (Gemini Direct, Bedrock `aws` + Vertex `gcloud-adc`, `gateway-compat`), and **Step 3B** (sandboxed `claude`/`gemini`/`codex` harnesses, MCP bridge + CONNECT proxy, `oauth2` device-code, `subscription_budget_tokens`, active probing) keeps all four D-19 access paths in v1 before Step 4 Councils, protects the D-12 Step 2 Go/No-Go gate, and guarantees the Step 3 OS sandbox and worktree gates exist before harnesses launch.
- **(c) Mid-Stream Crash Retry Accounting:** **Combine both: charge `reserved_usd` (or reserved tokens) on every interrupted call AND cap consecutive mid-stream crash retries at 2 per turn.**
  *Justification:* Charging the full reservation (`usage_source = 'estimated'`, `outcome = 'cancelled'`) on each interrupted call prevents silent budget overrun when a call completed server-side (Builder #8). Capping consecutive mid-stream crash retries at 2 per turn before marking `outcome = 'error'` and escalating (Red-Team P1-5) prevents a deterministic parser/network crash from burning the entire remaining mission dollar budget in uncounted retries—or looping infinitely on `subscription` endpoints where `reserved_usd == 0`.
